Utility Pay

Utility Pay is a mobile-first fintech concept designed to simplify how users manage and pay recurring utility bills through a centralized digital experience. The project explores how intuitive workflows, organized financial information, and timely reminders can reduce the effort involved in managing everyday expenses while helping users stay informed, organized, and in control of their monthly payments.

Role

UI/UX Designer

Timeline

2–3 Weeks

Tools

Figma

Project Type

Personal Project

The Challenge

Managing multiple utility bills across different service providers often leads to fragmented experiences, missed due dates, and unnecessary administrative effort. Users frequently switch between separate platforms to review bills, complete payments, and track payment history, making routine financial management more time-consuming than it needs to be.

Key Challenges:

  • Utility payments were spread across multiple platforms.
  • Users lacked a centralized overview of recurring expenses.
  • Due dates were difficult to monitor consistently.
  • Payment history was not easily accessible.
  • Everyday financial tasks required unnecessary effort.

Why This Matters

Recurring payments are a routine part of everyday life, yet the experience of managing them often feels fragmented. A centralized utility management platform helps users stay organized, reduces the likelihood of missed payments, and creates a more predictable financial routine through thoughtful design and clear information hierarchy.

Design Strategy

The design strategy focused on creating a streamlined payment experience that reduces friction while giving users complete visibility into their recurring utility expenses.


Design Priorities:

  • Centralize recurring utility payments.
  • Improve visibility of upcoming due dates.
  • Simplify bill review and payment workflows.
  • Reduce cognitive load through structured financial summaries.
  • Create an interface that encourages confidence and consistency.

Research & Insights

To better understand common utility payment experiences, I reviewed existing bill payment applications and digital financial services. The analysis revealed that while most platforms successfully facilitated transactions, fewer provided users with a clear overview of recurring expenses or effective tools for managing future payments.


Key Insights:

  • Users benefit from seeing all recurring bills in one place.
  • Payment reminders reduce missed due dates.
  • Financial summaries improve spending awareness.
  • Simple payment flows increase user confidence.
  • Clear organization reduces friction during routine financial tasks.

Ideation & Exploration

The exploration phase focused on simplifying the complete payment journey—from reviewing upcoming bills to completing transactions and tracking payment history. Different dashboard layouts and navigation structures were explored to ensure users could manage recurring payments with minimal effort while maintaining full visibility into their financial commitments.


Design Considerations:

  • Surface upcoming payments prominently.
  • Reduce steps required to complete transactions.
  • Keep financial information organized and easy to scan.
  • Support quick access to payment history.
  • Maintain consistency across recurring payment workflows.

Final Design

The final interface combines bill management, payment tracking, and financial summaries into a clean and intuitive mobile experience. Information is organized around the user's recurring payment journey, allowing essential actions such as reviewing upcoming bills, completing payments, and accessing payment history to be completed quickly and confidently.


Design Highlights:

  • Centralized bill management.
  • Streamlined payment experience.
  • Due date reminders.
  • Financial overview dashboard.
  • Consistent mobile design system.
  • Intuitive navigation.

Project Impact

The proposed solution demonstrates how thoughtful UX design can simplify recurring financial management by reducing friction, improving organization, and helping users maintain greater control over their everyday utility expenses.


Potential Outcomes:

  • Reduced likelihood of missed bill payments.
  • Faster completion of recurring payment tasks.
  • Improved visibility of monthly expenses.
  • Greater confidence in managing utility bills.
  • More organized financial routines.

Reflections

Designing Utility Pay reinforced the importance of creating financial experiences that prioritize simplicity and reliability. Unlike occasional financial interactions, utility payments are recurring tasks that users complete regularly, making efficiency and clarity essential. This project strengthened my understanding of designing fintech products that reduce cognitive load, streamline routine workflows, and support users through consistent, predictable interactions.
