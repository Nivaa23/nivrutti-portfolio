SnackStop ERP Dashboard

SnackStop ERP Dashboard is a conceptual enterprise management platform designed to simplify day-to-day business operations through a centralized administrative interface. The project explores how a well-structured dashboard can improve operational visibility by bringing together inventory, sales, customer insights, employee management, and reporting into a single, intuitive workspace that supports faster decision-making and greater efficiency.

Role

Lead Designer

Timeline

2–3 Months

Tools

Figma

Project Type

Dashboard

The Challenge

Managing daily business operations requires users to continuously switch between inventory, sales, employee records, customer information, and performance reports. As enterprise systems grow in complexity, important information becomes harder to locate, increasing the time required to complete routine operational tasks and reducing overall efficiency.

Key Issues Identified

  • Operational data was distributed across multiple modules.
  • Managers required quicker access to business-critical information.
  • Information-heavy dashboards increased cognitive load.
  • Monitoring business performance required unnecessary navigation.
  • administrative workflows lacked a clear operational hierarchy.

Why This Matters

Enterprise dashboards play a critical role in helping businesses make informed operational decisions. A well-designed ERP system reduces administrative effort, improves visibility into business performance, and enables teams to focus on decision-making rather than searching for information across multiple interfaces.

Design Strategy

The design strategy focused on creating a centralized workspace that presents operational information through a clear visual hierarchy while supporting the everyday workflows of business managers and administrators.


Design Priorities:

  • Centralize operational data into a unified dashboard.
  • Improve visibility of key business metrics.
  • Reduce navigation between frequently used modules.
  • Simplify complex administrative workflows.
  • Create a scalable design system for future ERP features.

Workflow Evaluation & Insights

To understand how enterprise users interact with management systems, I reviewed common ERP workflows and modern administrative dashboards. The analysis highlighted the importance of surfacing business-critical information first, organizing modules according to operational priorities, and reducing unnecessary context switching during routine management tasks.


Key Insights:

  • Managers prioritize quick access to operational metrics.
  • Dashboards should surface actionable information rather than raw data.
  • Consistent navigation improves efficiency across large systems.
  • Information hierarchy directly influences decision-making speed.
  • Enterprise users value predictability over visual complexity.

Ideation & Exploration

The exploration phase focused on organizing operational workflows into a dashboard that supports quick decision-making while reducing visual complexity. Different layout structures were explored to determine how inventory, sales, employees, customers, and reporting modules could be presented in a way that minimizes navigation and supports everyday business operations.


Design Considerations:

  • Prioritize business-critical metrics.
  • Reduce unnecessary clicks between operational modules.
  • Group related information into logical sections.
  • Maintain consistency across administrative interfaces.
  • Support both quick overviews and detailed analysis.

Final Design

The final dashboard provides a centralized operational workspace where business managers can monitor performance, manage resources, and access essential information from a single interface. Clear visual hierarchy, structured navigation, and modular layouts improve usability while supporting the complexity of enterprise operations.


Design Highlights:

  • Centralized ERP dashboard.
  • Operational performance overview.
  • Modular administrative interface.
  • Structured information hierarchy.
  • Consistent enterprise design system.
  • Responsive dashboard experience.

Project Impact

The proposed solution demonstrates how thoughtful dashboard design can improve operational efficiency by reducing complexity and providing business managers with immediate access to the information they need to make informed decisions.


Potential Outcomes:

  • Faster access to operational insights.
  • Reduced navigation across business modules.
  • Improved visibility into business performance.
  • More efficient administrative workflows.
  • Better decision-making through structured information architecture.

Reflections

Designing the SnackStop ERP Dashboard reinforced the importance of workflow design in enterprise products. Unlike consumer applications, ERP systems must support complex operational tasks while remaining predictable and efficient. This project strengthened my understanding of dashboard architecture, information prioritization, and designing interfaces that enable users to manage large volumes of business information with confidence.
