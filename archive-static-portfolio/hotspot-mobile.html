HotSpot Mobile

HotSpot is a mobile discovery platform designed to help users explore restaurants, cafés, bars, and nightlife experiences through an intuitive, location-based interface. The project focuses on simplifying venue discovery by combining personalized recommendations, curated collections, and contextual information into a seamless mobile experience that encourages confident decision-making.

Role

Lead Designer

Timeline

2–3 Months

Tools

Figma

Project Type

Mobile App

The Challenge

Finding the right place to dine or socialize often requires browsing multiple platforms, comparing reviews, checking locations, and searching through inconsistent information. This fragmented experience makes spontaneous decision-making difficult and increases the effort required to dicsover places that match a user's preferences.

Key Challenges:

  • Venue information was scattered across multiple platforms.
  • Users spent excessive time comparing options before making a decision.
  • Personalized recommendations were limited.
  • Important details such as ratings, location, and atmosphere were difficult to evaluate together.
  • The discovery experience lacked a clear and engaging flow.

Why This Matters

People often make dining and nightlife decisions in the moment. A well-designed discovery platform reduces friction by presenting relevant information in a clear, engaging, and contextual way, helping users spend less time searching and more time enjoying meaningful experiences.

Design Strategy

The design strategy centered on creating a mobile experience that makes venue discovery feel fast, intuitive, and enjoyable while giving users the confidence to make informed decisions.

Design Priorities:

  • Simplify venue discovery.
  • Highlight personalized recommendations.
  • Improve visibility of key venue information.
  • Reduce decision fatigue through organized content.
  • Create an engaging browsing experience suitable for everyday use.

Research & Insights

To understand how users discover restaurants and nightlife destinations, I reviews popular location-based platforms and food discovery applications. While these products offered extensive information, they often overwhelmed users with choices instead of helping them quickly identify suitable options.


Key Insights:

  • Users preferred curated recommendations over endless browsing.
  • Visual content significantly influenced venue selection.
  • Location, ratings, and atmosphere were primary decision factors.
  • Filtering options needed to remain simple rather than overwhelming.
  • Quick access to essential information improved decision-making.

Ideation & Exploration

The exploration phase focused on reducing cognitive load while making discovery feel engaging and effortless. Different navigation patterns, card layouts, and browsing experiences were explored to create a balance between rich visual content and functional usability.


Design Considerations:

  • Prioritize high-quality imagery.
  • Surface the most relevant venue information immediately.
  • Keep navigation simple and thumb-friendly.
  • Encouragge exploration without overwhelming users.
  • Maintain a visually energetic experience that reflects nightlife.

Final Design

The final interface combines vibrant visuals with a structured information hierarchy to create an engaging venue discovery experience. Large imagery, concise content, and intuitive navigation help users quickly evaluate destinations while maintaining a clean and mobile interface.


Design Highlights:

  • Personalized discovery feed.
  • Location-aware recommendations.
  • Rich visual presentation.
  • Streamlined navigation.
  • Consistent design system.
  • Mobile-first interaction patterns.

Project Impact

The proposed solution demonstrates how thoughtful information architecture and visual hierarchy can improve venue discovery while reducing the effort required to make confident decisions.


Potential Outcomes:

  • Faster venue discovery.
  • Reduced decision fatigue.
  • Higher engagement through a personalized recommendations.
  • Improved exploration of local businesses.
  • More enjoyable mobile browsing experience.

Reflections

HotSpot reinforced the importance of balancing visual appeal with functional usability. While discovery platforms naturally rely on compelling imagery, the challenge was ensuring that users could quickly access the information needed to make informed decisions. This project strengthened my understanding of mobile-first design, content prioritization, and creating engaging experiences that support real-world decision-making.
