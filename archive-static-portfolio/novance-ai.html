Novance AI

Novance AI is a conceptual AI-powered financial platform designed to simplify personal finance through intelligent insights, proactive assistance, and personalized recommendations. The project explores how artifical intelligence can reduce the complexity of financial management by transforming raw financial data into clear, actionable guidance that helps users make more confident financial decisions.

Role

UI/UX Designer

Timeline

4 Weeks

Tools

Figma

Project Type

Internship Project

The Challenge

Managing personal finances can quickly become overwhelming, especially when users are expected to interpret complex financial data, monitor expenses, track savings, and make informed decisions independently. While financial platforms provide access to data, they often stop short of helping users understand what the data means or what actions they should take next.

Key Challenges:

  • Financial information was difficult for users to interpret.
  • Users lacked personalized guidance based on their financial behavior.
  • Important financial insights were buried within complex dashboards.
  • Budgeting and financial planning required significant manual effort.
  • Users needed proactive recommendations rather than passive reporting.

Why This Matters

Good financial decisions depend on understanding, not just access to information. By combining artifical intelligence with thoughful user experience design, financial platforms can shift from simply displaying data to actively supporting users with relevant insights, timely recommendations, and meaningful guidance throughout their financial journey.

Design Strategy

The design strategy focused on creating an AI-assisted financial experience that simplifies decision-making while maintaining transparency, trust, and user control.

Design Priorities:

  • Transform financial data into actionable insights.
  • Surface personalized recommendations at the right time.
  • Reduce cognitive load through simplified information hierarchy.
  • Build confidence through clear and transparent interactions.
  • Design AI features that feel supportive rather than intrusive.

Research & Insights

To understand opportunities within AI-powered finance, I reviewed modern personal finance applications, digital banking platforms, and emerging AI experiences. While many products successfully collected financial information, fewer translated that information into practical guidance that users could easily understand and act upon.


Key Insights:

  • Users value recommendations that are easy to understand.
  • Financial dashboards often prioritize data over clarity.
  • AI should explain suggestions rather than simply present them.
  • Personalized insights are more valuable when delivered contextually.
  • Trust remains a critical factor when introducing AI into financial products.

Ideation & Exploration

The exploration phase focused on designing interactions where artificial intelligence supports, rather than replaces, user decision-making. Different dashboards layouts, recommendation models, and conversational interaction patterns were explored to ensure AI-generated insights remained transparent, relevant, and easy to understand.


Design Considerations:

  • Keep AI recommendations contextual and explainable.
  • Prioritize the most relevant financial insights.
  • Reduce unnecessary information overload.
  • Maintain user control over financial decisions.
  • Balance automation with transparency.

Final Design

The final interface combines modern financial management with AI-assisted guidance to create a streamlined and approachable user experience. Financial information is organized into clear sections, while personalized recommendations, budgeting tools, and spending insights are presented in a way that encourages informed decision-making without increasing complexity.


Design Highlights:

  • AI-powered financial insights.
  • Personalized recommendations.
  • Simplified budgeting experience.
  • Clear financial dashboards.
  • Transparent information hierarchy.
  • Mobile-first interaction design.

Product Impact

The proposed solution demonstrates how artificial intelligence can enhance financial decision-making by making complex information easier to understand and transforming financial data into actionable insights.


Potential Outcomes:

  • Increased confidence in financial planning.
  • Faster understanding of spending patterns.
  • Improved budgeting through personalized guidance.
  • Reduced cognitive load when reviewing financial information.
  • More engaging long-term financial management experience.

Reflections

Designing Novance AI challenged me to think beyond traditional financial dashboards and explore how artificial intelligence can create more supportive user experiences. The project reinforced that successful AI products are not defined by the technology itself, but by how effectively they help users understand information, make informed decisions, and maintain trust throughout every interaction.
