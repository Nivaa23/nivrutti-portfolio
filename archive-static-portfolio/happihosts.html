Event Management Web App

Happihosts

Happihosts is a digital event planning platform designed to simplify how people create, manage and celebrate memorable occassions. The project brings together invitations, guest management, vendor discovery, and event coordination into one cohesive experience, reducing the complexity of organizing celebrations while helping hosts stay in control from planning to execution.

Role

UI/UX Designer

Timeline

1-2 Weeks

Tools

Figma

Project Type

Internship Project

The Challenge

Planning an event often involves juggling multiple tools, vendors, guest lists, and communication channels. Hosts frequently switch between messaging apps, spreadsheets, invitation platforms, and vendor websites, creating a fragmented experience that increases effort and makes event management unnecessarily stressful.

Key Challenges:

  • Event planning relied on multiple disconnected platforms.
  • Guest communication was difficult to manage consistently.
  • Finding reliable vendors required significant manual effort.
  • RSVP tracking lacked visibility and organization.
  • Hosts needed a centralized workspace to manage every stage of an event.

Why This Matters

Successful events depend on organization, communication, and coordination. A unified planning platform reduces administrative effort, improves visibility into every stage of the event, and allows hosts to focus on creating meaningful experiences rather than managing logistics.

Design Strategy

The design strategy focused on creating a platform that simplifies event planning while maintaining flexibility for events of different sizes and purposes.

Design Priorities:

  • Centralize every stage of event planning.
  • Simplify guest invitations and RSVP management.
  • Improve vendor discovery through structured browsing.
  • Create a clean dashboard for tracking event progress.
  • Design an intuitive experience that minimizes planning complexity.

Research & Insights

To better understand the event planning journey, I explored existing invitation platforms, vendor marketplaces, and event management tools. While many products solved individual problems effectively, few provided a unified experience that supported the complete planning process from invitations to execution.


Key Insights:

  • Hosts frequently switched between multiple applications.
  • Vendor discovery lacked transparency and consistency.
  • RSVP management often became difficult as guest lists grew.
  • Event progress was difficult to monitor without centralized planning tools.
  • There was an opportunity to combine planning, communication, and vendor management into one experience.

Ideation & Exploration

The ideation process focused on simplifying the planning journey by reducing unnecessary steps and organizing related tasks into a single workflow. Multiple dashboard layouts and navigation strucutres were explored to ensure hosts could quickly access invitations, vendors, guest information, and event updates without feeling overwhelmed.


Design Considerations:

  • Minimize navigation between planning tasks.
  • Surface upcoming actions and pending confirmations.
  • Organize vendor information for quicker comparison.
  • Reduce cognitive load through progressive disclosure.
  • Maintain a friendly and celebratory visual language.

Final Design

The final interface combines event planning, guest management, and vendor discovery into a unified experience designed around clarity and simplicity. Information is grouped into dedicated workspaces, allowing hosts to monitor planning progress, communicate with guests, and coordinate vendors without relying on multiple tools.


Design Highlights:

  • Centralized planning dashboard.
  • Streamlined invitation workflow.
  • Organized vendor marketplace.
  • Real-time RSVP tracking.
  • Consistent component-based interface.
  • Mobile-friendly user experience.

Project Impact

The proposed solution demonstrates how a centralized event planning platform can simplify coordination while improving communication between hosts, guests, and service providers.


Potential Outcomes:

  • Reuced planning complexity.
  • Improved guest communication.
  • Better visibility into event progress.
  • Faster vendor discovery and comparison.
  • More organized event management from start to finish.

Reflections

Happihosts reinforced the importance of designing products that support complete user journeys rather than isolated tasks. The project challenged me to think beyond individual interfaces and focus on how multiple planning activities connect into one cohesive experience. It strengthened my understanding of workflow design, marketplace experiences, and creating products that reduce complexity through thoughtful information architecture.
