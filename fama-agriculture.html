Agriculture Web Platform

FAMA - Agricultural Website

FAMA is a website concept designed for an agricultural organization committed to advancing food security, sustainable farming, and agricultural innovation across Africa. The project explores hows thoughtful information architecture and visual storytelling can communicate the organization's mission, showcase its initiatives, and build trust among farmers, partners, investors, and stakeholders through a modern digital experience.

Role

UI/UX Designer

Timeline

2-3 Weeks

Tools

Figma

Project Type

Internship Project

The Challenge

Organizations working in agriculture often lead impactful initiatives, yet their digital presence fails to communicate that impact effectively. Information is frequently scattered across multiple pages, project outcomes lack visibility, and visitors struggle to understand the organization's mission, initiatives, and long-term goals. This limits engagement with potential partners, donors, and communities that rely on accessible information.

Key Challenges:

  • The organization's initiatives lacked a clear digital narrative.
  • Agricultural projects and sustainability efforts were difficult to explore.
  • Information hierarchy made important content less discoverable.
  • The website did not effectively communicate credibility or long-term impact.
  • Stakeholders lacked a centralized source of project information.

Why This Matters

For mission-driven organizations, a website is more than a marketing asset - it is a communication platform that builds credibility, educates stakeholders, and inspires collaboration. A well-structred digital experience helps organizations clearly communicate their purpose while encouraging trust, transparency, and long-term engagement.

Design Strategy

The design strategy focused on transforming complex organizational information into an engaging and accessible experience while reinforcing FAMA's mission and credibility.


Design Priorities:

  • Clearly communicate the organization's missions and impact.
  • Present agricultural initiatives through structured storytelling.
  • Improve navigation across projects, services and sustainability programs.
  • Build trust through a professional and approachable visual identity.
  • Design a scalable website capable of supporting future organizational growth.

Research & Content Strategy

To understand how agricultural organizations communicate their work online, I reviewed existing nonprofit and agriculture-focused websites. The analysis highlighted a common challenge: valuable information was often available but presented without a clear hierarchy or narrative, making it difficult for visitors to quickly understand the organization's impact.


Key Insights:

  • Mission statements were often buried within lengthy content.
  • Project outcomes lacked visual emphasis.
  • Sustainability initiatives were difficult to navigate.
  • Users benefited from structured storytelling rather than information-heavy layouts.
  • Clear navigation significantly improved content discoverability.

Ideation & Exploration

The exploration phase focused on creating a website that balances storytelling with usability. Rather than presenting information as isolated pages, the content was organized into a logical journey that introduces the organization, highlights its initiatives, and demonstrates measurable impact.


Design Considerations:

  • Structure content around the organization's mission and initiatives.
  • Prioritize high-impact projects on the homepage.
  • Create dedicated sections for sustainability efforts.
  • Improve readability through stronger visual hierarchy.
  • Encourage exploration through intuitive navigation.

Final Design

The final website combines editorial storytelling with a clean, modern interface to communicate FAMA's mission and initiatives effectively. A structured layout, consistent typography, and carefully organized content allow visitors to quickly understand the organization's work while exploring projects, partnerships, and sustainability programs with confidence.


Design Highlights:

  • Mission-driven homepage.
  • Structured project showcase.
  • Dedicated sustainability sections.
  • Clear content hierarchy.
  • Consistent visual language.
  • Responsive website experience.

Project Impact

The redesigned experience demonstrates how effective information architecture and visual storytelling can strengthen an organization's digital presence while improving communication with its stakeholders.


Potential Outcomes:

  • Improved visibility of agricultural initiatives.
  • Stronger credibility with partners and investors.
  • Increased engagement through intuitive content discovery.
  • Better communication of sustainability efforts.
  • A scalable foundation for future organizational growth.

Reflections

Designing FAMA reinforced the importance of storytelling in digital product design. Rather than focusing solely on interface aesthetics, the project challenged me to organize complex information into a clear, engaging experience that communicates purpose and builds trust. It strengthened my understanding of information architecture, content hierarchy, and designing websites that effectively balance organizational goals with user needs.
